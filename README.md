# Task 2: Phishing email analysis
## Sample Phishing Email

From: security-update@paypa1.com

Subject: Urgent: Your Account Will Be Suspended

Dear Customer,
We detected unusual activity in your account. Your account will be suspended within 24 hours unless you verify your information immediately.
Click the link below to verify your account:
http://paypal-secure-verification.com

Failure to verify your account may result in permanent suspension.

Thank you,

PayPal Security Team


# Phishing Indicators Found

1. Suspicious Sender Address: The sender's email address uses "paypa1.com" instead of the legitimate "paypal.com".

2. Urgent Language: The email creates panic by claiming the account will be suspended within 24 hours.

3. Suspicious Link: The provided URL does not belong to the official PayPal domain and may redirect users to a malicious website.

4. Request for Sensitive Information: The email attempts to trick users into providing personal or financial information.

5. Social Engineering: The attacker uses fear and urgency to pressure the customer into acting quickly without verifying the message.

6. Generic Greeting: The email uses "Dear Customer" instead of the recipient's actual name.

# Conclusion
The email contains several phishing characteristics, including a spoofed sender address, urgent language, suspicious links, and social engineering tactics. 
Users should avoid clicking on such links, verify the sender's identity, and report suspicious emails to the appropriate security team.

## Interview Questions and Answers

1. What is phishing?
Phishing is a cyberattack in which attackers try to steal personal or sensitive information by tricking people.

2. How can you identify a phishing email?
To identidy a phishing email you must check the sender's address, suspicious links, urgent language, spelling errors, and unexpected attachments.

3. What is email spoofing?
Email Spoofing is a technique in which the hacker makes minimal changes that is email is forged and sent so that people will think it came from an trusted and legitimate source. 

4. Why are phishing emails dangerous?
They can lead to data theft, financial loss, identity theft, and malware infections.

5. How can you verify a sender's authenticity?
By checking the sender's domain, contacting the organization directly, and verifying the email through official channels.

6. What tools can analyze email headers?
MXToolbox Header Analyzer, Google Message Header Analyzer, and Microsoft Message Header Analyzer.

7. What actions should be taken on suspected phishing emails?
Do not click links or open attachments, report the email, and delete it.

8. How do attackers use social engineering in phishing?
They manipulate emotions such as fear, urgency, trust, or curiosity to trick users into revealing information or performing unsafe actions.
